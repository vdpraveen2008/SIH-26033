## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Farmers often struggle to find suitable buyers, receive fair prices, and manage transportation. Dependence on limited purchasing channels and a lack of demand information can lead to reduced earnings and wastage of agricultural produce.

A system is needed to connect farmers with multiple buyers and distribute available produce according to actual market requirements.

## Proposed Solution
READY TO HARVEST is a digital platform connecting farmers and FPOs directly with consumers, retailers, hotels, and bulk purchasers.

Its core innovation is the Multi-Buyer Harvest Allocation Engine, which divides available produce among multiple suitable buyers based on quantity, quality, location, price, and delivery requirements.

For example, a farmer with 1,000 kg of tomatoes can receive suggested orders from multiple buyers instead of depending on a single purchaser.

Key Features

- Direct farmer-to-buyer connectivity.
- AI-based demand forecasting.
- Multi-buyer harvest allocation.
- Freshness-aware distribution.
- Transportation route optimization.
- Transparent price and net-earning calculation.
- Farmer-controlled order approval.

## Feasibility and Viability
- Technical: Uses existing web technologies and optimization algorithms.
- Economic: Utilizes existing transport services and collection centres.
- Operational: Supports individual farmers, FPOs, and bulk buyers.
- Scalability: Can expand across different crops and geographical regions.

Challenges and Solutions:

- Inaccurate predictions – Updated demand and market data.
- Quality differences – Quality grading and verification.
- Transport limitations – Multiple logistics providers.
- Poor connectivity – Lightweight interface and offline data entry.
- Price fluctuations – Regular price updates and transparent estimates.

## Impact and Benefits
- Economic: Better market access, more selling opportunities, and improved price transparency.

- Social: Supports small farmers, strengthens FPOs, and encourages digital agriculture.

- Environmental: Better delivery coordination, reduced unnecessary transportation, and lower risk of produce wastage.
